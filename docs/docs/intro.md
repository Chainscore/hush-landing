# Confidential execution for open markets

Hush privately finds competing quotes, sponsors the network fee, and settles directly from your wallet.

The result is simple: define what you want to receive, sign once, and let the route stay private until settlement.

## What Hush is for

- **Private intent** — your exact requirements are not broadcast as an open order.
- **Exact outcomes** — receive the amount you set or nothing moves.
- **Atomic settlement** — the route, conversion, and delivery resolve as one operation.
- **No new custody** — assets settle directly from and back to your wallet.

Use the protocol pages to understand the execution model, or head to the [community page](community) to find Hush channels.
